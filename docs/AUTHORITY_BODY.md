# Authority Body

Project Sanctuary is stewarded by Lucid Studios under the Department of
Agentic Research and Development.

In this repository, the Department of Agentic Research and Development is the
maintainer and research stewardship body for Project Sanctuary. It is not a
government agency, court, licensing board, medical authority, legal authority,
clinical authority, fiduciary, or certification authority.

## Maintainer Authority

The maintainer body may:

- define the public release boundary;
- decide what enters the admitted core executable lane;
- accept, reject, or redirect public issues and pull requests;
- publish release notes and security policies;
- hold or withdraw release candidates;
- route sensitive reports to private review.

The maintainer body may not, through this repository alone:

- grant professional authority;
- license a user to practice in a regulated domain;
- certify a CME, Operator, or user for legal, medical, safety, or fiduciary
  work;
- activate CME.Actual or Sanctuary.Actual;
- admit private GEL, SelfGEL, credentials, or custody records;
- authorize external action;
- bind a model or provider.

## Release Authority

Release authority is a repository and product-governance function:

```text
release authority = permission to publish a bounded public artifact
```

Release authority is not:

```text
runtime authority
provider authority
professional authority
identity authority
credential authority
action authority
GEL admission authority
Actual-state authority
```

## Department Posture

The Department of Agentic Research and Development provides a public label for
the research discipline used in this repository:

- closed-gate governance;
- receipt-bearing work;
- candidate residue discipline;
- distinction-safe release language;
- model-agnostic tool-body design;
- human review before authority-bearing transitions.

The department name must not be used to imply public-office status,
governmental authority, public licensure, legal representation, medical
practice, delegated custody, or emergency-service responsibility.

## Operator And User Authority

Users and Operators remain responsible for their own legal, professional, and
local obligations.

This repository may help demonstrate a governed tool body. It does not replace
professional judgment, local compliance review, security review, institutional
authorization, or human accountability.

## Governance Formula

```text
stewardship may publish
publication may invite review
review may create evidence
evidence may support a later gate
gate support is not a gate opening
authority opens only through a separate reviewed authority surface
```
