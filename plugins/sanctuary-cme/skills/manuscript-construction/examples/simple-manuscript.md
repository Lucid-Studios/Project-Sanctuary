# Tiny Light-and-Growth Note

We placed two trays of fast-germinating seeds in otherwise similar indoor conditions for seven days. One tray received twelve hours of lamp exposure per day and the other received four hours. The twelve-hour tray had visibly taller seedlings at the end of the observation period.

This proves that more light always causes plants to grow taller.

The observation involved two trays in one room and did not control cultivar variation, lamp spectrum, temperature gradients, watering error, or measurement error. We did not test whether the result generalizes to other species, light intensities, or growth durations.
